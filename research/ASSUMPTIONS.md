| A3 | u.gg / lolalytics / Mobalytics terms forbid automated collection like OP.GG's do. | Could not retrieve u.gg's terms page. Against: some sites tolerate light crawling (OP.GG's help centre reportedly says it "does not prohibit" crawling with attribution).
